I’m a product designer in Kuala Lumpur. Since 2024 I’ve designed and shipped e-commerce storefronts and websites, and on the most recent one I built the store as well: the information architecture, the product taxonomy, the vehicle-fit search and the custom Shopify sections.

At AT Auto Shoppe I replaced a templated catalogue site with a Shopify store of my own design. Before that I ran web design for Precious Wedding’s campaigns, and through studio.mael I designed the PlanetStewards website and identity for a research project at Utrecht University.

I came to design from medicine. I studied at the Faculty of Medicine, Alexandria University, from 2011 to 2017 before leaving to read Interface Design at Multimedia University in Cyberjaya, graduating in 2023. Medicine left me with a grounding in observation that I still use at the start of every project.
